# Project Title

## Problem Statement
Fields that require the crossbreeding of plants may take months to develop relevant discoveries, with a lot of theoretical planning, with chances for nature to take it's own unexpected turn. When trying to create specific crossbreeds of plants, there may not even be a guarantee for the outcomes of experimentation.

## Solution
Simulating genetics in plants, our program will take the sets of traits from two individual plants, and provide the possibilities of crossbreeding them. With access to a widespread database on different plant species, with relevant information on their traits, our program will also match it's theoretical suggestions to currently known plant species, and give a % accuracy to its calculations. This can save lots of time with crossbreeding plants, and make it easier to plan a road map.
