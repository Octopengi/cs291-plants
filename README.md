# Untitled Plant Breed

## Problem Statement
Fields that require the crossbreeding of plants may take months to develop relevant discoveries, with a lot of theoretical planning, with chances for nature to take it's own unexpected turn. When trying to create specific crossbreeds of plants, there may not even be a guarantee for the outcomes of experimentation.

## Solution
Simulating genetics in plants, our program will take the sets of traits from two individual plants, and provide the possibilities of crossbreeding them. With access to a widespread database on different plant species, with relevant information on their traits, our program will also match it's theoretical suggestions to currently known plant species, and give a % accuracy to its calculations. This can save lots of time with crossbreeding plants, and make it easier to plan a road map.

## Why is this valuable?
With the ability to easily find ways to crossbreed into a specific species of plant, researches can save time in their planning and get right to work on obtaining the correct plant.

## How will we make money?
This software will be available for purchase, either through a one time purchase, individual user license, or through a monthly company wide subscritpion, allowing numerous employees access to the software under one fee.

## MVP Features
1. Dataset of scientifically named plants, and their traits that are relevant in determining their crossbred children
2. The option to select two plant species out of the database, and calculate what plant species are most likely to come of their crossbreeding
3. Lookup up a plant species that has been crossbred in the past, and see what the parent species of that plant is
